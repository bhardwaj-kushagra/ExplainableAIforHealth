inference (DLNM-style models). Results provide actionable thresholds
for public health early warning systems.

METHODOLOGY NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- ML Model: XGBoost with count:poisson objective
- Explainability: SHAP (TreeExplainer)
- Causal Inference: Stratified analysis with bootstrap CI
- Data: Synthetic 3-year daily admissions + meteorology
- For production use, replace with real hospital data.

LIMITATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Current model uses synthetic data for demonstration.
- Confounding by air pollution (PM2.5) included but may be incomplete.
- Spatial heterogeneity not modeled (single-region analysis).
- Recommend validation with external hospital datasets.
